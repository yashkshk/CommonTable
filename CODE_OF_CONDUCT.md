# Code of Conduct

CommonTable welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected conduct

Be respectful, give specific and actionable feedback, assume good intent when reasonable, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Respect others' privacy and time. Credit contributions accurately.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private information, and repeated disruptive or insulting behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using a private contact method on their GitHub profile. Avoid repeating private or abusive material in public issues. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban participants according to the severity and pattern of conduct. Reports should be handled with discretion; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where legal obligations apply.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained for the project.
